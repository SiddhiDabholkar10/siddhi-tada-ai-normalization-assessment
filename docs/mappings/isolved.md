## Company fields

- One company per legal (D-034). The client is the parent (Company.parent). source_ids hold the client id and the legal id; the canonical id uses the legal id only (D-036).
- Each legal has one tax entity: its id is built from the legal id (D-042), its name is the legal name and its EIN is federalEIN.
- Deduction and earning codes come from the client pay item catalog (D-066): Deduction items become deduction_codes; Earning and Memo Calc items become earning_codes. Every legal of the client gets the same catalog, because the catalog is client-level.
- Work location addresses are typed as work addresses (AddressType work); the company address as mailing.
- No W-2 box or code is sent for pay items: w2_box and w2_code are null with not_provided. calc_method, rate_class and quantity_type likewise.

## Employee fields

- One Employee per isolved employee id (D-039); the legal employee list (paged, D-013) is the main source. The detail route is read only for what the list does not return, employeesWithSSN only for the SSN (D-057, D-070).
- Step 3a covers profile, employment, job and compensation; step 3b-1 the list's taxes, i9Documents and reciprocityRule and the i9/status route. Step 3b-2: contacts, accruals, skills, education, certifications, training and priorEmployment. Step 4 (D-081): directDeposits[] is excluded; the directdeposits route is the deposit source.
- Related people and qualifications (step 3b-2): one RelatedPerson per contacts item (its flags say dependent, beneficiary or emergency contact); one Qualification per skills, education, certifications or training item, typed by the route. The masked twins (ssn, birthDate) are excluded and the unmasked fields mapped (D-020, D-071).
- PTO (step 3b-2): one PtoBalance per accruals item, with plan_source_id and plan_name only. Amounts without a documented unit (currentBalance, availableBalance, carryoverBalance, ytdAccrued, ytdTaken) stay raw in provider_extensions with a unit_unverified diagnostic, and the hours fields are not_provided (CANONICAL_MODEL 1.5, D-077). accruals/list is excluded (D-079).
- Tax withholding (step 3b-1): taxes.federal is the federal setting (level federal, jurisdiction 'US', residency not_applicable); residentState and nonResidentState are state settings (residency resident and non_resident, from the block name; jurisdiction taxState); residentCity and nonResidentCity are local settings (residency from the block name; the locality is not sent, so jurisdiction is not_provided). An absent or null block adds no setting. isolved sends no exempt claim: is_exempt is not_provided. Filing status codes take the labels isolved sends with the same codes on check details (taxCategories[].filingStatus); codes never seen with a label are unknown.
- Work authorization (step 3b-1): i9_completed and i9_completed_date from the list's i9Documents (the date part of a timestamp, the full value kept in sensitive_provider_extensions, CANONICAL_MODEL 1.6); hasSignedI9Form and uploaded documents from i9/status to sensitive_provider_extensions; document A, B and C details from the detail route (D-070). document_expiration_date, is_nonresident_alien and visa_type are not sent on these routes (not_provided).
- employment_history holds two dated series: status entries from statuses (status set, employment_type not_provided) and employment category entries from categories (employment_type set, status not_provided). Each keeps its employment_id (D-039).
- job_history comes from the jobs route (D-069); 404 means no records (D-011).
- Termination date, type and reason describe the current employment: not_applicable when the employment status is not terminated, with values still sent kept in provider_extensions (CANONICAL_MODEL 3.2, D-080).
- Current pay comes from the list. Pay frequency and the pay group id come from the salary entry in effect: the latest effectiveDate among entries with isInactive false. For 6 of 6 sampled employees that entry's pay group name, per-pay salary and hourly rate equal the list's.
- Organization fields are client-defined (titles such as Location and Department): the org unit type is unknown (unmapped) with the title as raw_code, never read from the title's words.
- Labels sent with each record (gender, ethnic origin, marital status, citizenship, termination type, EEO category, pay type, salary frequency) are the provider's labels and are matched exactly; an unlisted label is unknown with an unknown_code diagnostic.
- Currency is not sent; USD is assumed (CANONICAL_MODEL 1.5).
